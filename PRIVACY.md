# Diwali Utsav Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Diwali Utsav is a mixed reality celebration experience for Meta Quest, provided by XRGEEK. It lets you place and light virtual diyas, use sparklers, and enjoy virtual fireworks in your physical surroundings.

This policy explains how the app processes information and how you can manage or request deletion of that information.

## Information Used by the App

### Mixed reality and interaction data

Diwali Utsav uses Meta Quest headset and controller tracking, and hand tracking when enabled, to position the view and let you interact with virtual objects. Tracking information is used during play; the app does not create a recording of your hand or body movements.

With your spatial-data permission, the app reads available room scene information, including floor height, room dimensions, and window locations. This helps align virtual items with the floor and position and adjust celebration sounds. The app releases the loaded room scene after using this information. It does not save a separate copy of your room mesh or upload your room scene to XRGEEK.

The app uses the headset's passthrough capability to display your surroundings behind virtual content. Diwali Utsav does not record, save, or transmit passthrough images or videos.

### Saved placements and preferences

The app stores identifiers for placed items and spatial anchors so that persistent items, such as diyas, can return to their saved positions in later sessions. Anchors are saved through Meta Quest's local spatial-anchor functionality; the app does not use cloud anchor sharing.

The app also stores preferences such as music mute status and the position of the virtual selection tray. These records are stored on your device. Other temporary gameplay state is used while you play.

### Information you send us

If you email us for support or a privacy request, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request. Please avoid sending room images or other sensitive information unless necessary for your request.

## Accounts, Microphone, and Advertising

Diwali Utsav does not require a separate XRGEEK account. The app does not record microphone audio or provide voice chat or voice-note recording.

We do not sell personal information or use app data for advertising. Diwali Utsav does not include advertising or developer-operated behavioral analytics. XRGEEK does not operate a server that receives the room data, tracking information, saved placements, or preferences described above.

## Meta Quest and Other Service Providers

Diwali Utsav uses Unity and Meta XR software to run the experience. Meta independently operates the headset, user accounts, store, permissions, and platform services. Information handled by Meta through those services is subject to Meta's privacy policies and your platform settings.

If you contact us by email, our email service provider processes that correspondence to deliver and store it. We do not otherwise send your locally stored gameplay data to third parties. We may disclose support correspondence when legally required.

## Storage and Retention

Saved placements and preferences remain on your device until removed or changed. The app does not offer its own cloud synchronization service. Any platform-managed backup or device service is controlled separately by the platform and its settings.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve your request, maintain necessary support records, or meet legal obligations. XRGEEK cannot remotely access or erase information held only on your headset.

## Your Controls and Data Deletion

- Use the app's **Reset** control to clear the celebration and request deletion of the saved spatial anchors currently loaded by the app. Reset does not clear every preference or Meta's system room setup.
- Remove the app's local preferences and app data through your device's app-data controls, where available, or by uninstalling the app. Platform-managed spatial data and backups may require separate controls in your headset or account settings.
- Manage spatial-data and tracking permissions in Meta Quest settings. Revoking a permission can affect the corresponding feature.
- For help deleting data or to request deletion of support correspondence, email [muj.amrut@gmail.com](mailto:muj.amrut@gmail.com) with the subject **Diwali Utsav Data Deletion**. This request route is available regardless of where you live. We will delete information we hold unless retention is legally required, and help you identify the relevant controls for device-only information.

## Younger Users

Use of Diwali Utsav must follow Meta's age, account, and parental-permission requirements and applicable law. We do not ask children to provide personal information in the app. A parent or guardian may contact us about privacy or deletion using the address below.
