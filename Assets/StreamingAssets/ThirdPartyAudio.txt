Every recording below is CC0, CC BY 3.0 or publisher-designated Public Domain.
The music "A Little Light", the launch jingle and the remaining interface, flame,
lighter, anar, chakri and phuljhadi sounds are original to this project.

"Firecracker with wick #4" by Joseph SARDIN, LaSonotheque.org:
https://lasonotheque.org/petard-a-meche-4-s1140.html
CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Changes: excerpt 0.0456-1.494 s with 2 ms before the attack, gain 0.85,
sub-millisecond attack de-click and final 60 ms fade. Output is 1.448 seconds,
mono 48 kHz PCM. Used alone for the bomb report, synchronized to the burst flash.

"Interface Sounds - click_001" by Kenney (www.kenney.nl):
https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
Download mirror: https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds
Creative Commons Zero 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Changes: mono 48 kHz PCM conversion, fixed gain 0.32 and short tail fade.
Used as the short, dry cracker selection click; no pitch or reverb added.
